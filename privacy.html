ShiftSheet

Privacy Policy

Last updated 3 September 2026 · Applies to the ShiftSheet Employee Timesheet & Shift Schedule Google Sheets add-on

The short version. This add-on has no server. It runs inside Google Sheets on Google's own infrastructure. We do not receive, store or see your spreadsheet, your staff names, their hours or their pay. The add-on itself makes no network calls at all: nothing is sent to us, and nothing is sent to any third party. This is described in full below.

Who we are

ShiftSheet is an independent add-on published by an individual developer. Contact: support@shiftsheet.me.

What the add-on can access

The add-on requests one permission from Google, and Google classes it as non-sensitive. It exists for one job:

PermissionGoogle classificationWhat it allowsWhy it is needed
spreadsheets.currentonly Non-sensitive Read and write only the single spreadsheet you have open while the add-on is running. To build the schedule grid and write the report sheets.

Because that one permission is non-sensitive, the "Google hasn't verified this app" screen should not appear when you open your copy. If you do see it, click Advanced, then Go to ShiftSheet (unsafe), then Allow.

Because the spreadsheet permission is currentonly, the add-on cannot see your other spreadsheets, your Google Drive, your Gmail, your Google Calendar, your contacts, or anything else in your Google Account. This is enforced by Google, not by us.

The only Google user data the add-on accesses is the content of the single spreadsheet you have open at the time: the staff names, shift codes, hours and rule settings you type into it. That data is read in order to build the schedule and run the checks, it is written back to the same spreadsheet, and it is used for nothing else. It is never sent to us, never stored outside your own Google Account, and never shared with anyone. We access, use, store and share no other Google user data.

What we collect

We do not collect, receive, store or transmit your spreadsheet content. We operate no servers, no database, and no analytics.

The add-on saves five small pieces of information using Google's own storage service (PropertiesService), which keeps them inside your Google Account and the document you are working in. We cannot read any of them, and none of them is ever sent anywhere:

  • Your rule settings — maximum weekly hours, minimum rest, overtime threshold and similar. Stored in the document.
  • Which month the schedule covers, so the reports know what to add up. Stored in the document.
  • Which sheets the add-on created itself, so that it never overwrites a sheet you made. Stored in the document.
  • How many schedules you have finished, counted so the add-on can ask once whether you would leave a review. Stored against your Google Account.
  • Whether that review question has already been answered, so it is never asked a second time. Stored against your Google Account.

Outbound requests: none

The add-on makes no network calls. It contacts no server of ours, because we run none, and it contacts no third party either. Nothing is sent when you install it, nothing is sent while you use it, and nothing is sent when you buy the paid version.

The paid version is a separate copy of the template, so there is no key to enter and no check to run. Whether a copy is the free or the paid one is decided by a line in its own code, inside your own Drive.

Google's Limited Use requirements

ShiftSheet's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Specifically: we do not transfer your Google user data to third parties except as necessary to provide or improve the add-on; we do not use it for advertising; we do not sell it; and no humans read it, because it never reaches us in the first place.

There is no exception to this. No Google user data, and nothing derived from it, is transferred anywhere by the add-on, because the add-on makes no network calls.

Data retention and deletion

Since we hold no copy of your data, there is nothing on our side to retain or delete.

  • To remove your saved rule settings, delete the sheets the add-on created, or delete the spreadsheet itself.
  • To revoke the add-on's access entirely, uninstall it, or visit your Google Account permissions.
  • Everything the add-on wrote into your spreadsheet stays there afterwards as ordinary cells, readable without the add-on installed.

Children

This add-on is a business tool and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children — or, indeed, from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the date at the top of this page changes with it. If a change ever meant the add-on started collecting data it does not collect today, that would require a new permission, and Google would ask for your consent before it took effect.
